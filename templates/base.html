Zero-Cloud Guarantee

Your privacy is fundamental. This PC Performance Monitor runs 100% locally on your machine:

  • No telemetry or logs are ever transmitted over the Internet.
  • All hardware sensor queries (psutil, py-cpuinfo, pynvml) run natively in your local Python environment.
  • Performance metrics are stored exclusively in your local SQLite database (pc_monitor.db).
  • All fonts, scripts, and chart utilities are bundled and served offline.
